Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

What we access

When a creator authorizes DailyCut Studio, we receive an access token and basic profile information (open id, display name, avatar) for that account only. We use them solely to upload video and photo drafts the creator asked us to prepare.

What we store

Access and refresh tokens are stored locally on the creator's own computer and are never shared, sold or sent to third parties. We do not collect data about viewers, followers or other users.

Content

The videos and images we upload are created by the creator's own production tool. Drafts are reviewed and published (or deleted) by the creator in the app.

Revoking access

A creator can revoke access at any time from the app's settings (Security and permissions, Apps and services). Revoking access stops any further upload; locally stored tokens can be deleted at any time.

Contact

For any privacy question, contact the creator account that uses this tool through its profile.
