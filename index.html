DailyCut Studio

DailyCut Studio is a content production tool for short educational and news videos and photo posts in Italian (video game news and general curiosities, for several pages).

It prepares vertical videos and photo carousels and sends them as drafts to the creator's own account. Nothing is posted automatically: the creator opens the draft in the app, reviews it, edits the caption and privacy settings, and decides whether to publish.

The tool only accesses the accounts that explicitly authorize it, and only to upload drafts.
